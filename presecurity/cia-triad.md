## What I did
Completed The CIA Triad room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about the CIA Triad - Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability -
as the core framework that protects the digital world, and how cybersecurity
as a whole is fundamentally a security mindset built around these three
principles.

## What clicked
Understood that almost every security concept covered so far in Pre Security
maps back to protecting one (or more) of these three things - e.g. weak
passwords threaten Confidentiality, tampered logs threaten Integrity, and a
DDoS-style attack threatens Availability.

## Why this matters for security
The CIA Triad is the foundational lens SOC analysts use to assess the
severity and nature of any incident - when investigating an alert, part of
the job is identifying which of the three (or more than one) is at risk,
which shapes how urgently and how it should be handled.

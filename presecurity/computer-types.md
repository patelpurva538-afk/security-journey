## What I did
Completed the Computer Types room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about different categories of computers - smartphones, servers, laptops,
desktops, embedded chips, and IoT devices like smart lights, smart fridges, and
smart cameras - and how each is suited to a different purpose based on efficiency
and power needs.

## What clicked
Understood why the same "computer" concept looks so different across devices -
a server is built for power and constant uptime, while an embedded chip in a
smart light is built to be small and efficient for one simple task.

## Why this matters for security
Every device type is a potential target with its own weaknesses. IoT devices
(smart cameras, fridges, lights) are notoriously under-secured because they're
built cheap and simple, making them common entry points for attackers into a
home or company network. Servers, by contrast, are usually more locked down
but are higher-value targets. Understanding device types helps a defender know
where to expect weak points across a network.

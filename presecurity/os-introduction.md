## What I did
Completed the Operating Systems: Introduction room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about the operating system's kernel space, how different companies
choose different OS types based on their needs, the difference between GUI
and CLI-based operating systems, different OS versions, and how different
devices use different operating systems suited to their purpose.

## What clicked
Understood that the OS works invisibly as the middle layer connecting hardware,
applications, and the user - most of what it actually does happens behind the
scenes without the user ever seeing it directly.

## Why this matters for security
The kernel is the most privileged part of a system - if an attacker gains
kernel-level access, they control the entire machine, which is why kernel
security is treated as critical. Understanding how the OS mediates between
hardware and applications also helps explain why OS-level security settings
and patches matter so much for defenders trying to prevent that level of access.

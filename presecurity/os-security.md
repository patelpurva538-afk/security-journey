## What I did
Completed the Operating System Security room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about logging into a system with different credentials/passwords,
checking login history, and the difference between weak and strong passwords
- what makes a password easy or hard to crack.

## What clicked
Understood that login history isn't just a record - it's actual evidence a
defender can check to spot unauthorized access, like unexpected login times
or unfamiliar accounts being used.

## Why this matters for security
Weak passwords remain one of the most common ways attackers gain initial
access to a system, which is why password strength and login monitoring are
basic but critical defensive measures. Checking login history is a real
investigative step a SOC analyst takes when trying to determine if an account
was compromised.

## What I did
Completed the Alert Triage With Elastic room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Used Elastic queries to investigate alerts across different scenarios,
the same triage job as the Splunk room but inside a different SIEM.

## What clicked
Doing triage in both Splunk and Elastic showed me that the skill is the
investigation process, not the tool. The interface and query syntax
change, but the steps stay the same: read the alert, search for related
activity, decide if it's a true or false positive, and prepare to report it.

## Why this matters
Real SOC teams use different SIEMs, and job postings name Splunk, Elastic,
or others. Having triaged alerts in both means I can say in an interview
that I've done the workflow on two platforms and can pick up a third.

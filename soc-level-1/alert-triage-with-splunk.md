## What I did
Completed the Alert Triage With Splunk room on TryHackMe, a scenario-based
room where I triaged real-style alerts inside Splunk.

## What I learned
Worked through three alert types in Splunk: an initial access alert, a
persistence alert, and a web shell alert. For each one, I had to investigate
in Splunk and decide what the alert meant.

## What clicked
This combines a lot of earlier material into the actual Tier 1 workflow. The
SOC Alert 1 Triage room taught the process of judging and routing an alert,
Detecting Web Shells taught what a web shell looks like, and the Splunk rooms
taught how to search. Initial access and persistence are also stages from the
Cyber Kill Chain and MITRE ATT&CK, so I was seeing those frameworks show up
as real alerts instead of definitions.

## Why this matters
This is the closest room so far to the job I'm training for: get an alert,
investigate it in a SIEM, decide if it's real, and be ready to report it
using the Five W's method from Alert Reporting. Having triaged three
different alert types across the attack lifecycle is good portfolio evidence
of applied skill.

## What I did
Completed the Benign room on TryHackMe, a scenario-based investigation in
Splunk where I searched by user and host to work out what happened.

## What I learned
Used Splunk queries filtered by user and host to investigate a scenario. This
was one of the harder rooms, because I had to decide what to search for
myself instead of following guided steps.

## What was difficult
The scenario didn't tell me where to look. I had to combine what I knew
about Windows logs, Splunk queries, and attacker behavior, and it took more
than one attempt at the queries to get useful results.

## Why this matters
This is the same skill Tier 1 work demands: start from a suspicious host or
user, query the SIEM, and follow the evidence. It builds on Alert Triage
With Splunk, Windows Threat Detection, and Living Off The Land Attacks. Being
able to say "this one was hard and here is how I worked through it" is
honest and credible in an interview.

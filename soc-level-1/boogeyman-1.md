## What I did
Completed the Boogeyman 1 room on TryHackMe, part one of a multi-part
investigation series.

## What I learned
Started the investigation from a phishing email, analyzing the email headers
and then moving into network traffic analysis to trace what happened after
the email was opened.

## What clicked
This pulled the Phishing Analysis module (header checks, sender
verification) together with the Network Traffic Analysis module (tracing
what the malicious content did once delivered). It's the natural next step
after Phishing Analysis Fundamentals and The Greenholt Phish, showing what
happens after the phishing email itself is confirmed malicious.

## Why this matters
Boogeyman is a well-known TryHackMe investigation series, and having it on a
GitHub portfolio is a recognizable signal to anyone in the field who has
also done TryHackMe content. Starting from a phishing email and following it
into network traffic mirrors a real intrusion investigation from initial
access onward.

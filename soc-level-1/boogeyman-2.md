## What I did
Completed the Boogeyman 2 room on TryHackMe. Not a direct continuation of
Boogeyman 1's case, but a similar style of investigation and a step deeper
in difficulty.

## What I learned
Worked through a separate scenario using the same investigation approach as
Boogeyman 1 (phishing origin, header/network analysis), applied to a new
case rather than the same one.

## What was difficult
This was harder than Boogeyman 1, even though the investigation style was
familiar. The specific scenario required more effort to work through.

## Why this matters
Seeing the same investigation pattern (phishing entry point, deeper
analysis) applied to a new case, and finding it harder than the previous
one, shows the series is designed to build difficulty room by room rather
than being one long single case. That's useful pattern-recognition
practice, not just a memorized single scenario.

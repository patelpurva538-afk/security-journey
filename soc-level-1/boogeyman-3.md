Finishing all three Boogeyman rooms, each harder than the last, is a good
marker of real progress - phishing/network investigation skills that felt
difficult in Part 1 became the baseline for tackling something harder in
Part 3. This kind of increasing-difficulty series is exactly what a
portfolio should show: not just completion, but growth in the ability to
handle harder cases.

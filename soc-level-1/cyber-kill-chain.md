## What I did
Completed the Cyber Kill Chain room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about the Cyber Kill Chain - the different stages an attacker moves
through during an attack (from initial reconnaissance to final actions on
objectives) - and how breaking an attack down into stages helps defenders
understand and intervene at any point along the chain.

## What clicked
Understood that the Kill Chain and Pyramid of Pain work together but from
different angles - the Kill Chain maps WHEN in an attack something happens
(the stages), while the Pyramid of Pain maps WHAT type of indicator is
found and how much it hurts the attacker to lose it. Together they give a
fuller picture for analyzing an incident.

## Why this matters
Breaking an attack into stages means a defender doesn't need to catch
everything at once - stopping an attacker at any single stage (e.g.
blocking during reconnaissance or during the delivery stage) can prevent the
full attack from succeeding. This is a core mental model for incident
response and threat detection.

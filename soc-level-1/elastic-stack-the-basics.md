## What I did
Completed the Elastic Stack: The Basics room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Got hands-on with the Elastic Stack, using Kibana dashboards to search and
filter data with queries - a similar workflow to Splunk, but a different
SIEM tool.

## What clicked
Recognized the same underlying pattern from Splunk showing up again with
different terminology and interface - ingest data, search/filter it, view
results on a dashboard. This reinforced that SIEM tools share a common
purpose even when the specific product differs.

## Why this matters
Elastic Stack (ELK) is another widely-used SIEM platform, often chosen as a
free/open-source alternative to Splunk - including for home lab setups.
Having hands-on exposure to both Splunk and Elastic makes me more adaptable,
since real SOC jobs vary in which tool they use, and the core skill (query
and filter logs to find what matters) transfers between them.

## What I did
Completed the Humans as Attack Vectors room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about phishing mitigation and detection, company security policies,
employee security awareness programs, and how to spot fake emails and
malicious links.

## What clicked
Understood that humans are often the weakest link in security, not the
technology itself - which is why awareness training and clear company
policies are treated as seriously as technical defenses like firewalls or
antivirus.

## Why this matters
This sets up the Phishing Analysis module coming later in SOC Level 1 -
recognizing fake emails and links is a core investigative skill, and
understanding why humans are targeted (rather than just how) helps explain
why phishing remains one of the most common attack methods companies face.

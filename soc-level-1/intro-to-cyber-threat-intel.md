## What I did
Completed the Intro to Cyber Threat Intel room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) as a core input into SIEM
triage - how threat intel feeds and data connect to MITRE ATT&CK to give
context to alerts, rather than investigating each alert in isolation.

## What clicked
This ties the SOC Workbooks and Lookups room (which introduced threat intel
lookups briefly) together with MITRE ATT&CK in more depth - CTI is what
actually populates those lookups with meaningful, structured information
about known threats, techniques, and indicators.

## Why this matters
Good threat intel is what turns a raw alert into an informed decision - CTI
provides the context (is this IP/domain/technique associated with a known
threat group?) that lets a Tier 1 analyst triage faster and more accurately,
directly feeding into the SIEM Triage skills this module is building toward.

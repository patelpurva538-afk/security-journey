## What I did
Completed the Introduction to EDR room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned what EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response) does - collecting
telemetry (detailed activity data) from endpoints (individual devices),
going beyond traditional antivirus by monitoring behavior continuously
rather than just scanning for known malware signatures.

## What clicked
Understood the difference between EDR and basic antivirus - antivirus mainly
checks files against known threat signatures, while EDR watches ongoing
behavior on a device, which is what allows it to catch new or unusual
threats that don't match a known signature yet.

## Why this matters
EDR telemetry is a major source of the alerts a SOC analyst triages daily -
understanding what data EDR collects and why helps explain where alerts
come from in the first place, connecting directly to the SOC Alert Triage
room from earlier in this path.

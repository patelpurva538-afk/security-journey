## What I did
Completed the Introduction to SIEM room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned what SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) does - it
pulls together logs and alerts from many different devices into one central
dashboard, solving the problem of logs being scattered across individual
systems with no unified way to see suspicious activity across the whole
network.

## What clicked
Understood how SIEM and EDR fit together - EDR generates detailed telemetry
from individual endpoints, and SIEM is the central system that aggregates
that data (along with logs from other sources) into one place, which is
what a SOC analyst actually looks at during triage.

## Why this matters
SIEM is the actual dashboard tool behind everything covered so far - alert
triage, workbooks, playbooks - it's the central hub where a SOC analyst's
day-to-day work happens. Understanding what SIEM solves (fragmented,
scattered log data) explains why it's considered essential SOC
infrastructure.

## What I did
Completed the Introduction to SOAR room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response) - how
it automates repetitive response actions using playbooks, connecting
different security tools together to act automatically instead of requiring
a human to manually perform every step.

## What clicked
This directly connects to the AI-automation shift discussed earlier - SOAR
is exactly the kind of technology automating routine Tier 1 response actions
(like auto-blocking a known-bad IP), which is why the analyst role is
shifting toward reviewing and validating automated actions rather than
performing every step manually.

## Why this matters
Understanding SOAR isn't optional anymore for an entry-level analyst -
knowing how playbooks automate response, and being able to review/validate
what the automation did, is becoming a core part of the modern SOC role
rather than a separate advanced topic. This ties SIEM (detection), EDR
(endpoint data), and SOAR (automated response) into the full modern SOC
toolchain.

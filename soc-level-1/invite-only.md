## What I did
Completed the Invite Only room on TryHackMe - a capstone-style scenario for
the threat intel content in this module.

## What I learned
Applied the threat intel skills from the previous few rooms together in one
combined investigation - using file/hash, IP, and domain threat intel
together rather than in isolation, similar in structure to the Summit and
Shadow Trace capstone rooms from earlier modules.

## What clicked
Recognized the recurring pattern across SOC Level 1 - each module builds
individual skills room by room, then ends with a combined scenario that
forces using all of them together, which mirrors how a real investigation
actually unfolds (you don't know in advance which single skill will solve
the case).

## Why this matters
This reinforces threat intel as a toolkit rather than a single skill -
knowing when to check a hash versus an IP versus a domain, and combining
findings from all three, is what a real Tier 1 investigation into a
suspicious file or connection actually requires.

## What I did
Completed the MITRE room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about the MITRE ATT&CK framework - a knowledge base of attacker
tactics, techniques, and sub-techniques, including how to look up specific
technique IDs, threat groups, associated software, and detailed information
about how each attack works.

## What clicked
Understood how MITRE ATT&CK ties together everything from the last few
rooms - the TTPs from the Pyramid of Pain and the stages from the Kill
Chain/UKC are exactly what MITRE ATT&CK catalogs in detail, giving specific,
searchable IDs and real-world examples for each technique instead of just
abstract concepts.

## Why this matters
MITRE ATT&CK is an industry-standard reference used constantly in real SOC
work - when investigating an alert, mapping it to a specific ATT&CK
technique ID gives a precise, shared language for describing what happened,
which connects directly to writing clear reports (from the Alert Reporting
room) and to the Wazuh home lab project planned later, since Wazuh
explicitly maps detections to MITRE ATT&CK.

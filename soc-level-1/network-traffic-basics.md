## What I did
Completed the Network Traffic Basics room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned how network traffic actually works in practice, building on the
Wireshark introduction from Phishing Prevention - covering how traffic
moves across a network and can be captured and observed.

## What clicked
This ties directly back to Packets & Frames and the OSI Model from Pre
Security - those rooms explained the theory of how data moves through
layers, and this room starts putting that theory into practice by actually
observing real traffic flow.

## Why this matters
Understanding normal network traffic behavior is the essential baseline for
the rest of this module - a defender can only spot abnormal traffic
(malicious activity) by first being solidly familiar with what normal
traffic looks like.

## What I did
Completed the NetworkMiner room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Got introduced to NetworkMiner - a tool that extracts files, parameters, IP
and MAC addresses, connection details, and message data directly from a
packet capture, presenting it in a more organized, readable way than raw
Wireshark output.

## What clicked
NetworkMiner felt more approachable than the Wireshark Traffic Analysis room
- instead of manually parsing raw packets, it automatically organizes and
extracts the useful information (files, connections, messages), which made
some of the same underlying data from Wireshark easier to understand from a
different angle.

## Why this matters
Having multiple tools for the same job matters - NetworkMiner's automatic
extraction is useful for quickly pulling files or connection details during
an investigation, complementing Wireshark's more manual, detailed packet-by-
packet inspection. Different tools suit different investigation needs.

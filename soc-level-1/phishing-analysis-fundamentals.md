## What I did
Completed the Phishing Analysis Fundamentals room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned the basic steps to analyze a suspicious email - checking the sender
address for spoofing signs, inspecting links before clicking, examining
attachments for malicious content, and reading email headers for hidden
routing information.

## What clicked
This directly puts the Humans as Attack Vectors concepts into practice -
that room explained WHY phishing works and why humans are targeted, and
this room provided the actual HOW: the concrete technical steps to verify
whether a suspicious email is genuinely malicious or safe.

## Why this matters
Phishing analysis is one of the most common daily tasks for a Tier 1 SOC
analyst, since phishing remains one of the top attack vectors. Having a
structured checklist (sender, links, attachments, headers) turns "does this
look suspicious?" into a repeatable, defensible investigation process.

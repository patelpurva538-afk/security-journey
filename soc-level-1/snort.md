## What I did
Completed the Snort room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Got hands-on with Snort, a real IDS/IPS tool, and reinforced the
NIDS/HIDS/NIPS/HIPS distinctions (network vs host-based, detection vs
prevention systems) with a concrete, widely-used tool as the example.

## What clicked
Snort made the abstract IDS Fundamentals concepts concrete - instead of just
knowing the categories (NIDS, HIDS, NIPS, HIPS) in theory, seeing Snort as
an actual signature-based tool that inspects traffic and generates alerts
based on defined rules made the distinction between detection (IDS) and
prevention (IPS) clearer.

## Why this matters
Snort is a real, commonly referenced tool in SOC job postings and industry
discussions, so hands-on exposure here is directly relevant experience.
Understanding rule-based detection also connects back to signature-based
detection from IDS Fundamentals - Snort essentially works by matching
traffic against a defined ruleset, similar to how antivirus matches file
signatures.

## What I did
Completed the SOC Alert 1 Triage room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned how to identify and read logs, distinguish true positives from false
negatives, and how alerts get categorized and assigned - priority levels
like critical, high, low, and statuses like in-progress, closed, or none -
along with understanding why a specific alert gets flagged (whether it's a
real threat or a false alarm).

## What clicked
Understood that triage isn't just "look at alert, react" - it's a structured
decision process: read the alert's details, judge if it's a true or false
positive, assign the right priority, and route it correctly (to yourself or
another analyst) based on severity.

## Why this matters
This is the actual hands-on Tier 1 analyst workflow - the exact job I'm
preparing for. Distinguishing true vs false positives is one of the most
critical skills in the role, since misjudging either wastes time on nothing
or misses a real threat. This directly builds on the log-reading skills from
Linux/Windows CLI Basics in Pre Security.

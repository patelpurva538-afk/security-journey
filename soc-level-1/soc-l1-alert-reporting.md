## What I did
Completed the SOC L1 Alert Reporting room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned how to write clear comments and reports when escalating an alert to
an L2 analyst, how to send proper details in different report formats, and
the "Five W's" method (who, what, when, where, why) for structuring an
incident report.

## What clicked
Understood that reporting isn't just paperwork - a poorly written handoff to
L2 wastes their time re-investigating what L1 already found, so the Five W's
method exists specifically to make escalation fast and complete the first
time.

## Why this matters
This directly follows the triage room - once you've judged an alert as a
true positive needing escalation, writing a clear, structured report is what
makes the SOC team function efficiently as a whole, not just as isolated
analysts. This is a core communication skill for the actual job.

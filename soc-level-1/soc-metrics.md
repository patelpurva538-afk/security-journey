## What I did
Completed the SOC Metrics room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about key SOC metrics - MTTD (Mean Time to Detect), MTTA (Mean Time
to Acknowledge), and MTTR (Mean Time to Respond/Resolve) - the differences
between them, how delays in each are measured, and what percentage targets
and reporting expectations look like, including who these metrics get
reported to.

## What clicked
Understood that these three metrics track different stages of the same
incident timeline - how fast a threat is spotted (MTTD), how fast someone
acknowledges it (MTTA), and how fast it's actually resolved (MTTR) - so a
delay in any one stage points to a different part of the process needing
improvement.

## Why this matters
These metrics are literally how SOC team performance is measured and
reported to management - understanding them means understanding what a SOC
analyst's work is actually judged on, not just the technical triage/response
skills themselves.

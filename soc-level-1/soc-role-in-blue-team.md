## What I did
Completed the SOC Role in Blue Team room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned how different types of attacks happen and who responds to each based
on their position within a SOC team, including how SOC structures differ
across contexts like an MSSP (Managed Security Service Provider) versus an
in-house bank SOC team.

## What clicked
Understood that "SOC analyst" isn't one uniform job - responsibilities and
escalation paths shift depending on the type of organization (MSSP serving
multiple clients vs a dedicated in-house team like a bank's), and different
roles within the team handle different stages of an incident.

## Why this matters
This builds directly on Junior Security Analyst Intro by adding structure -
knowing WHO handles WHAT and WHERE (MSSP vs in-house) is essential context
for understanding where an entry-level role like mine would fit, and what
kind of company/environment I might be working in after graduation.

## What I did
Completed the SOC Workbooks and Lookups room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about threat intel lookups (checking indicators like IPs or file
hashes against threat databases), playbooks (standardized response
procedures for specific alert types), and workbooks used to track and assign
tasks - including how a case gets handed off to an L2 analyst.

## What clicked
Understood that playbooks exist so analysts don't have to reinvent the
investigation process for every alert - if it's a known alert type, there's
a documented step-by-step procedure to follow, making triage faster and more
consistent across the whole team.

## Why this matters
This ties directly into the earlier triage and reporting rooms - playbooks
are the actual tool that guides HOW you triage, and workbooks/task assignment
are how the escalation-to-L2 process from Alert Reporting actually gets
tracked and managed day to day. This is the operational backbone of a
functioning SOC.

## What I did
Completed the Splunk: The Basics room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Got hands-on with Splunk's actual dashboard - adding a data file (JSON
format), reviewing and naming a search, and writing basic search queries to
look through the ingested data.

## What clicked
Seeing SIEM concepts from the previous room turn into an actual working tool
- Splunk is a real, widely-used SIEM platform, and searching/filtering data
through queries is exactly the "pulling logs into one place" idea from
Introduction to SIEM, now made concrete and hands-on.

## Why this matters
Splunk is one of the most common SIEM tools used in real SOC jobs, so
hands-on familiarity with it directly translates to on-the-job skill.
Writing search queries here is conceptually similar to the SQL WHERE/SELECT
logic from Pre Security - filtering large datasets down to what matters.

## What I did
Completed the Summit room on TryHackMe - the capstone challenge for the
Cyber Defence Frameworks module, with 6 different levels of difficulty.

## What I learned
Applied the frameworks learned throughout this module together in one
scenario - working through indicators like IPs, hashes, and domains across
increasing difficulty levels, using the Pyramid of Pain, Kill Chain, and
MITRE ATT&CK concepts in combination rather than isolation.

## What clicked
Going through 6 levels of increasing difficulty showed how these frameworks
aren't separate tools to pick from - a real investigation uses all of them
together, moving from simple indicators (IPs, hashes) up to understanding
the broader attack pattern and technique behind them.

## Why this matters
This is the first time everything from Cyber Defence Frameworks came
together in one connected exercise rather than one concept per room - much
closer to what a real investigation actually feels like, combining
indicator analysis with the bigger-picture frameworks to understand not
just what happened, but how and why.

## What I did
Completed the Systems as Attack Vectors room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about technical attack vectors including supply chain attacks, USB
Rubber Ducky-style hardware attacks, CVEs (Common Vulnerabilities and
Exposures), and how strong IT policy, training, and consistent system/app
updates help defend against these weaknesses.

## What clicked
Contrasted directly with Humans as Attack Vectors - that room covered
tricking people, while this one covers exploiting technical weaknesses
(unpatched systems, vulnerable supply chains, malicious hardware). Together
they cover the two main categories every real attack falls into: people or
systems.

## Why this matters
CVEs and unpatched systems are a constant real-world attack path - understanding
this is why patch management and vulnerability tracking are core SOC/IT
responsibilities. Combined with the previous room, this gives the full picture
of the two broad ways organizations get breached, both of which a SOC analyst
needs to recognize and respond to.

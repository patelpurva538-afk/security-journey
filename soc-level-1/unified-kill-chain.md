## What I did
Completed the Unified Kill Chain room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Learned about the Unified Kill Chain (UKC) and its different phases - a
model similar to the original Cyber Kill Chain but expanded, offering a
more detailed breakdown of attacker behavior across a full intrusion.

## What clicked
Understood that UKC builds on the same foundational idea as the Cyber Kill
Chain (breaking an attack into stages to enable intervention at any point),
but addresses some of the original model's gaps by covering more phases in
more detail - showing how security frameworks evolve and improve over time
rather than staying fixed.

## Why this matters
Having both models gives more flexibility when analyzing an incident -
some organizations or tools may reference the original Kill Chain, others
UKC, so understanding both means being able to work with whichever framework
a team or job actually uses.

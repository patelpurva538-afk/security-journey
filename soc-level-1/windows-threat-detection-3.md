## What I did
Completed the Windows Threat Detection 3 room on TryHackMe - the final part
of this 3-part series.

## What I learned
Covered C2 (Command and Control) communication detection, backdoors,
privilege groups, and further malware/trojan identification, rounding out
the Windows threat detection skill set.

## What clicked
Looking back across all three parts, there was a clear progression - Part 1
covered foundational threats (USB, phishing), Part 2 went deeper with
Sysmon and privilege escalation, and Part 3 covers the more advanced
persistence/C2 stage, mirroring the later stages of the Cyber Kill Chain
(command and control, actions on objectives).

## Why this matters
C2 detection is one of the most valuable skills for stopping an attack
before real damage occurs - if a defender can spot backdoor/C2 traffic,
they can cut off an attacker's control before further stages of the Kill
Chain play out, tying this whole 3-part series back to the frameworks
learned earlier in SOC Level 1.

## What I did
Completed the Wireshark: The Basics room on TryHackMe.

## What I learned
Got hands-on with Wireshark - opening a packet capture, applying different
filters to narrow down traffic, and inspecting individual packets in detail,
seeing TCP/UDP and OSI model concepts reflected directly in real captured
data.

## What clicked
Everything from Pre Security's Packets & Frames, TCP/UDP, and OSI Model
rooms finally became visible and concrete - instead of reading about frames
and packets abstractly, I could see actual packets in Wireshark and
recognize which OSI layer and protocol each one belonged to.

## Why this matters
Wireshark is one of the most widely used tools in real SOC and network
security work - being able to open a capture and filter down to relevant
traffic is a core practical skill for the Network Traffic Analysis module,
and it's also the exact tool planned for the home lab project later to
verify the Wazuh brute-force detection.

## What I did
Independently practiced Wireshark on my own PC, analyzing sample DNS and
HTTP packet capture files (dns.pcap, http.pcap) outside of any guided
course or room.

## What I learned
Worked through Wireshark's core features and filtering system in depth -
capture filters, display filters, following streams, and inspecting
individual packets across DNS and HTTP traffic specifically.

## What I found
Became confident using Wireshark's filters to isolate relevant traffic
quickly (e.g. filtering to just DNS queries or just HTTP requests) rather
than scrolling through raw captures. Not yet at an advanced level, but
comfortable and capable with the core workflow.

## Why this matters
Wireshark is one of the most commonly used tools in real network traffic
analysis and incident investigation - this builds directly on the
Wireshark rooms from SOC Level 1 (Basics, Packet Operations, Traffic
Analysis) by practicing independently on self-chosen sample files, which
shows initiative beyond guided coursework and reinforces a skill that
felt difficult the first time through.
